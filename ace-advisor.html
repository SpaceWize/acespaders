What it covers

Six ways to work with Ace

1

Personal and Professional Coaching

Intuitive, deeply tailored sessions that cut through surface noise with sharp, unexpected questions and just the right mix of humor, movement, or stillness. Whether over a drive, a walk, target practice, gym work, quiet reflection, or fine dining, we uncover your own breakthroughs in relationships, health, career, or life direction. 2-5 hour immersive experiences designed for real, lasting clarity. 2

On Location Representation and Scouting

Boots on the ground to assess complex situations or determine the facts in your stead, ensuring opportunities unfold with your long-term vision. Get a second set of hand-eye-ears on an endeavour or project you’re considering or undertaking already. Ask to be represented in negotiations or at events by someone who has confidence in achieving desired results. Whether locally or abroad, you can be certain that you have an Ace up your sleeve, working in your best interest.

3

Health and Fitness Sessions

This is not personal training, fitness training, or a visit with a nutritionist. Gain for yourself a new perspective on what it means to be fit and healthy, helping to dissolve mental blocks. Have someone join you at the gym, on walking/hiking trails, or other custom outlets, so you’re not alone as you get started. Get tips and tricks to keep you motivated as you go through your personal health and fitness journey. This bundles great with the Cybertruck services and other Add-Ons.

Crisis Management Consulting

Get direct, grounded guidance when facing down sudden, life-altering business or personal situations or decisions. Direct, no-bull responses, focused on rapid recovery, prevention, and actionable steps forward. Similar to the On Location Representation and Scouting service, but with more urgency or necessity. Best paired with the On-Call Problem-Solving Add-On for potentially prolonged situations requiring recurring consults.

5

Group or Corporate Strategy Sessions

Get an outside perspective on your corporate project or problem, with an all-in-one marketing, sales, and team advisor. Another set of eyes on what you’re too close to can be the difference between a breakthrough or a breakdown. See the About Page for more information about Matthew and why he might be able to help, or fill out the form to ask him directly.

Holistic Life Optimization Packages

Comprehensive programs merging personal, professional, relationship, and health advising into a full-suite elevation plan. Customized 2-5 hour-long immersive sessions, with ongoing mentorship and check-ins to ensure profound, sustained transformation. Sessions can be in various formats and locations, depending on your customized package choices: online, drive, walk, gym, quiet space, fine dining, etc.
