How it runs

Four steps, no theatre

  1. The first conversation

    An honest hour on what is actually happening, what has been tried, and whether Ace is the right person for it. If he is not, he will say so.

  2. Terms & scope

    A clear remit, a defined cadence and an agreed way to measure whether it worked. Most engagements settle onto a monthly retainer.

  3. On the ground

    Regular check-ins, sessions that end in decisions, and on-call advice when something moves. For extended partnerships Ace will relocate temporarily to be genuinely on site.

  4. The hand-back

    The point is to leave something that holds without him. Systems, people and decisions documented well enough that the next quarter runs on its own.

Structure

Monthly retainer by default. Extended partnerships and defined project work also available.

Location
