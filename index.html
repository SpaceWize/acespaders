About

Ace — aka
Matthew

Hi, I’m Matthew Ferguson, known online as Ace Spaders.

I’m a husband, father of three, and builder who turns hard-earned experience into measurable results.

For nearly two decades I’ve helped people navigate major challenges, from personal crises and relationship resets to professional pivots and business scaling.

I’m now selectively opening a small number of additional spots for those facing significant issues, projects, or opportunities where real breakthroughs are needed.

My approach is straightforward: I deliver planned action and clear results with no rigid scripts and no superficial advice. I listen deeply, ask questions that cut to the core, and adapt every engagement to what’s actually required. I aim to solve the problem thoroughly so you can move forward decisively, while always staying open to repeat business or longer-term partnerships when they make sense.

We work in the environments where breakthroughs happen: a quiet sit, a drive-and-talk, a walk, target shooting, gym work, fine dining, or from the Cybertruck as my mobile fortress. I also provide on-location representation and scouting. I do boots-on-the-ground work assessing complex situations, representing clients in negotiations or at events, and identifying real estate and business opportunities (recently across Vancouver Island, Japan, North BC, and the Fraser Valley).

I’m President and Director of Make Life Better Events Inc. while actively building other businesses, hosting and planning events, and creating content. My time is fiercely protected, my wife and our three homeschooled kids come first, and our family rhythm forms the foundation for everything I do.

I’m not a licensed financial advisor or lawyer, but I stay obsessively current on markets, trends, and opportunities. I’m happy to share practical insights, foresight from real-world calls, and straight talk, including when it makes sense to bring in specialists.

Boardrooms are welcome too. I prefer face-to-face for highly invested sessions and leave most admin work to others. That’s not why people come to me. I may represent your interests, but I won’t work as an employee at your company.

Sessions typically run 2-5 hours because that’s where real progress occurs. Online is available at a lower rate, but in-person commands the premium. Many problems trace back to overlooked environmental or relationship factors, so I often start with a quick audit there.

Availability is limited by design. Family, current clients, and ongoing builds take priority, so spots are exclusive and high-ticket. If you’re ambitious, driven, and ready to invest in genuine elevation through focused, high-impact work, reach out via the form.

Let’s see if we’re the right fit.
