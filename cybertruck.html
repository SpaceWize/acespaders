Seven ways to book it

Private Sessions in the Cybertruck

Speed up your personal or professional growth with confidential, in-person coaching or consulting inside the Cybertruck. Tackle relationships, health, or business challenges with proven foresight and problem-solving. Premium rates ensure exclusivity and effectiveness. Mobile Event and Networking Space

Summon a versatile networking unit with a host for crowd control at client or staff events, intimate meetings, or branded pop-up events for both private and public needs. Leverage its mobility and provide a USP for your group activities. Ideal for professionals building partnerships on location, where it matters most. Great in combo with a Custom Coffee/Snack Add-On

3

Mobile Vending Services

Mobile coffee/snack vending with customizable menus and Add-Ons (company-funded vending packages or patron-paid options available). Perfect for events, team perks, big family and friend outings, or on-site construction crew refreshments. (Courtesy of you? ;) Order the truck as a convenient, refreshment hub; Giant Phone Charger/ Boombox?

Premium Pickup & Transport

High-profile executive transport and pickup service. Get chauffeured for the day or week by an Ace or picked up in the Cybertruck for seamless, luxurious one-off travels. Ideal for local professionals, spontaneous date nights, airport runs, or en route strategy talks when paired with various Add-Ons. Combine safety and exclusivity with planned-out practical convenience.

Film and Production Hires

Rent the Cybertruck for indie, commercial, or music video shoots. Use it as a striking set piece, production vehicle for crew transport, or for versatile equipment use cases. Its unique design and mobility add edge to your project, with flexible setups for seamless integration.

Use the Cybertruck as a standalone studio for podcast recordings, talking-head videos, or quick promo shoots. Film inside the truck. Equipped for creators needing a versatile, on-location setup without traditional studio hassles.
