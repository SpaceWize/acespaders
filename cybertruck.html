On Location

In The Truck

Scouting, immersive sessions and boots-on-the-ground work across British Columbia and beyond. Some problems only make sense once you are standing in the place they are happening.

01 — The idea

The office moves

A call tells you what someone thinks the problem is. Standing on the site tells you what it actually is. The truck exists so that distance stops being a reason to work at arm’s length.

It carries the work to wherever it needs to happen — a location being assessed, a site being scouted, a business that needs someone physically present for a week rather than a scheduled hour.

BC and beyond. If it can be driven to, it is in range.

02 — On the road

What the truck is for

01

On-Location Scouting

Sites assessed in person. Access, logistics, power, permissions and the honest read on whether the place actually works for what you have planned.

02

Immersive Sessions

Extended time working the problem together, on site, rather than an hour on a call. Where the real decisions tend to get made.

03

Boots On The Ground

Physical presence when something needs a person there — a transition, an event load-in, a situation that has stopped responding to phone calls.
